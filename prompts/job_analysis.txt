only what the posting actually says. Do not guess at a salary, seniority
level, or requirement that is not stated or clearly implied by the text

or a URL.

the title, location, work arrangement, or seniority is not explicit but is
clearly implied by the text, give your best reading and prefix it with
"(inferred) ". "summary" is one plain sentence describing the role. Keep
"tools_and_keywords" to terms genuinely useful for understanding the job
(skills, tools, domain terms), not generic filler words.
