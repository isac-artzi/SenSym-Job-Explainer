Community Program Coordinator — Bright Path Youth Alliance

Bright Path Youth Alliance runs after-school tutoring and mentorship programs
across twelve partner schools. We're hiring a Community Program Coordinator
to manage day-to-day operations for our tutoring sites on the east side of
the city.

In this role you will:
- Be the main point of contact for six site locations, coordinating
  schedules between tutors, school staff, and families.
- Recruit, screen, and onboard volunteer tutors, including running a short
  orientation session each month.
- Track attendance and program outcomes in our shared spreadsheet system and
  prepare a monthly summary for the program director.
- Handle logistics: supply orders, room bookings, transportation
  coordination for special events.
- Respond to parent questions and concerns with warmth and follow-through.
- Represent Bright Path at two or three community events per semester —
  school fairs, library partner nights, that kind of thing.

You'll thrive here if you:
- Have experience working with kids or in a school, camp, or youth-program
  setting (paid or volunteer both count).
- Are organized enough to keep six sites straight without dropping a ball,
  but flexible enough when a site's schedule changes on short notice.
- Communicate clearly and warmly with people from a lot of different
  backgrounds — parents, tutors, school administrators, kids.
- Are comfortable with everyday tools: email, shared spreadsheets, a group
  messaging app.
- Have reliable transportation between sites; a valid driver's license is
  required.

A bachelor's degree is preferred but not required if you have equivalent
experience. We especially welcome applicants who are bilingual in Spanish
and English, since a large share of the families we serve speak Spanish at
home.

This is a full-time, hybrid role: two days a week in our central office, the
rest split across site visits. Bright Path offers a supportive, mission-
driven team and real room to shape how this program grows.
