Junior IT Support Analyst — Rivergate Health Systems

Rivergate Health Systems is a regional network of outpatient clinics looking
for a Junior IT Support Analyst to join our four-person help desk team. This
is an entry-level, on-site role based at our Millbrook office.

What you'll do:
- Answer help desk tickets and walk-in requests from clinic staff — password
  resets, printer issues, slow computers, and the occasional "the scanner is
  making a weird noise."
- Set up new laptops and badge-in workstations for new hires using our
  imaging tool.
- Keep our internal knowledge base articles up to date as you learn new
  fixes.
- Escalate networking and server issues to our senior infrastructure team
  and follow up until they're resolved.
- Rotate through a light on-call schedule (about one week every six weeks)
  for after-hours emergencies at the clinics.

What we're looking for:
- Comfortable troubleshooting Windows 10/11 and basic networking (you know
  what a DNS server does, roughly).
- Some experience with a ticketing system (Zendesk, Freshdesk, or similar)
  — a class project counts.
- A CompTIA A+ certification, or actively working toward one.
- Patience. Half the job is explaining the same fix to a stressed nurse in a
  calm voice.
- Reliable transportation to our Millbrook office; this is not a remote
  position.

Nice to have:
- Familiarity with HIPAA and why we're careful about patient data on shared
  workstations.
- Any exposure to Active Directory user management.

This is a great first IT job for someone who wants hands-on experience
before specializing in networking, security, or systems administration.
Rivergate promotes from within, and two of our current senior analysts
started on this same help desk team.

Rivergate Health Systems is an equal opportunity employer.
