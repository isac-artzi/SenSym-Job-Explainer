Staff Machine Learning Platform Engineer — Northgrove Analytics

Northgrove Analytics builds forecasting tools for mid-size logistics
companies. We're looking for a Staff Machine Learning Platform Engineer to
lead the team that keeps our model training and serving infrastructure fast,
reliable, and boring in the best way — nobody should ever have to think
about it unless something's wrong.

You will:
- Own the architecture of our internal ML platform: feature store, training
  orchestration, model registry, and online serving.
- Set technical direction for a team of five engineers, including reviewing
  designs and mentoring two engineers who are newer to distributed systems.
- Partner directly with the applied research team so new model architectures
  make it from notebook to production without a six-week integration slog.
- Drive down training and inference cost without regressing latency SLAs —
  this is a standing, ongoing responsibility, not a one-time project.
- Represent the platform team in quarterly planning with engineering
  leadership.

What you bring:
- 8+ years in software or ML infrastructure engineering, with at least 3 of
  those years owning a platform other teams depend on.
- Deep hands-on experience with distributed training (data or model
  parallelism) and with serving infrastructure at meaningful scale.
- A track record of technical leadership — not necessarily formal
  management, but designs other engineers have built on.
- Strong Python and comfort reading/writing performance-sensitive code in a
  systems language when the situation calls for it.
- Judgment about when to build versus buy; we are a lean infra team and
  can't own everything ourselves.

Nice to have:
- Experience specifically in logistics, supply chain, or another
  operations-heavy forecasting domain.
- Familiarity with cost-attribution tooling for shared GPU clusters.

This role reports to the Director of Engineering and is one of two Staff
positions on the infrastructure org. Northgrove is a Series C company of
about 140 people; the platform team has grown from two to five engineers in
the last year and this hire is meant to give the team a clear technical
anchor as it keeps growing.

Northgrove Analytics offers a hybrid arrangement out of our downtown office,
with two in-office days expected per week.
